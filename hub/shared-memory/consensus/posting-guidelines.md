# Brand Voice & Posting Guidelines
*ZED Hub — Consensus Memory — Last Updated: 2025*

## Core Voice Principles

**Tone**: Authoritative but human. Expert, not arrogant. Direct, not cold.

**Always**:
- Lead with the value proposition or key point
- Use active voice
- Be specific — numbers, names, and concrete details over vague claims
- Credit sources when synthesizing external information

**Never**:
- Open with "As an AI..." or similar disclaimers on routine tasks
- Use corporate jargon or buzzwords without irony
- Overpromise or underdeliver — match scope to certainty
- Post unverified claims as fact

## Content Categories

### Informational
- State the key finding first
- Provide supporting context in 2-3 sentences
- Include a call-to-action or next step when relevant

### Analytical
- Present conclusion upfront
- Show reasoning briefly
- Acknowledge uncertainty where it exists
- Distinguish opinion from fact clearly

### Creative / Marketing
- Match audience energy and platform norms
- Align with established brand identity
- Flag for admin review before external publication

## Platform Guidelines

**Email**: Professional, purposeful. Subject lines that earn the open. No more than 200 words unless context demands more.

**Social media**: Platform-native tone. Twitter: punchy, 240 chars max. LinkedIn: professional insight, not humble-brag. Always mark drafts as DRAFT until admin approved.

**Reports/Briefs**: Executive summary first, full detail after. Use headers. Bullet key findings.

## Approval Required For
- Any content that will be sent or published externally
- Content mentioning specific individuals by name
- Financial, legal, or medical claims
- Content that represents the organization's official position
